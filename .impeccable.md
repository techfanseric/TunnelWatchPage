## Design Context

### Users

TunnelWatch is currently a personal operations tool used by its owner to monitor proxy subscription sources, routes, renewals, and related spending across web and Android. Billing records are stored in the cloud and should be shareable. The product should preserve a clear path toward multiple users and multiple devices even though account switching and collaboration are not part of the first release.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, mechanical, and quietly characterful. The interface should feel dependable and purpose-built, with the precision of an operations instrument and the tactile memory of a freshly printed receipt.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing light operational dashboard and introduce billing as a clean white thermal receipt, without yellowed paper, distressed vintage effects, or excessive realism. Each saved expense should feel like another section has just advanced from a compact receipt printer. On the web, the receipt occupies a fixed left rail and never joins the masonry flow. On Android, billing is the third primary tab after subscription sources and routes.

### Design Principles

1. Make the receipt metaphor structural: use perforation, print rhythm, tabular figures, and incremental reveal only where they clarify billing history.
2. Keep the surface white and contemporary; personality comes from typography, spacing, and motion rather than sepia, heavy shadows, or decorative clutter.
3. Optimize the main job: record a new purchase or renewal quickly, including payer, fixed RMB amount, dates, and a finite or unlimited period.
4. Treat sharing and ownership as first-class data concerns so the model can later expand to multiple users and devices.
5. Preserve TunnelWatch's compact operational density and accessible interaction states across desktop and mobile.
